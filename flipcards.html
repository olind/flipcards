Flip cards: glosor

Generates flip cards from a word list. The format is: english - swedish

How to print

  1. Click Download PDF and open the file (it opens in Preview on a Mac). Choose File → Print, set scale to 100% (not "Scale to fit"), and choose pages 1 to 1. Print.
  2. Take the printed sheet and turn it over sideways, like a page in a book. Keep the top of the page at the same end as before.
  3. Put it back in the paper tray and print pages 2 to 2.
  4. Cut along the dashed lines.

First time with your printer? Pencil a small arrow pointing up on a blank sheet, put it in the tray, and print page 1. Where the arrow ends up shows whether the tray prints on the side facing up or down, and which end goes in first. Then you know how to put the sheet back for page 2.
